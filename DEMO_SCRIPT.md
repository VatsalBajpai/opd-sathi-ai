# OPD Saathi — 2-minute submission demo script

**0:00–0:15 — Problem + product**  
“Patients often struggle with four separate problems: choosing the right hospital, getting there, finding the right place inside, and understanding the queue. OPD Saathi connects that journey in one accessible platform.”

**0:15–0:42 — Real hospital discovery**  
Sign in as a patient. Open **Best hospital nearby**, choose **ENT** or **Emergency**, enable live location, and show real hospital results, evidence labels, distance/ETA and the road map. Say: “Capabilities are never invented—unknown public data stays unknown.”

**0:42–1:05 — Verified + 3D navigation**  
Open **Verified Hospitals** briefly to show source-linked real hospital maps. Then open **Find a department**, switch to the 3D navigator, select Cardiology, and show the animated entrance → lift → department route plus accessibility/safety markers. Say clearly: “The 3D hospital is our simulated routing demo; real hospital maps are shown only when verified.”

**1:05–1:25 — Saathi AI**  
Ask: “What does an ECG measure? Explain simply.” If configured, show the real AI answer. Mention English/Hindi/Kannada support and that the API key stays server-side.

**1:25–1:45 — OPD token**  
Open **My visit & token**, enter a demo patient name, generate a token, and show queue position/wait estimate.

**1:45–2:00 — Staff + close**  
Switch to `staff@opdsaathi.demo`, call the next token, then close:  
“OPD Saathi is the patient journey layer—from current location, to the right hospital, to the right room, to the right queue, with accessible navigation and AI help throughout.”
